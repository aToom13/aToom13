### 👨‍💻 About Me

I am an engineering student and passionate **Systems & Robotics Builder** working at the intersection of embedded hardware, autonomous systems, and modern developer tooling.

- ⚡ **Daily Driver:** Proud **CachyOS** user (performance-tuned Arch Linux) with a customized Wayland / Hyprland desktop workflow.
- 🚀 **Flagship Project:** Developing and refining **[AtomCLI](https://github.com/aToom13/AtomCLI)** — a modern, extensible command-line ecosystem built to accelerate workflows.
- 🤖 **Robotics & Hardware:** Prototyping autonomous ground vehicles (**UGV**), designing custom PCBs with **KiCAD**, and programming microcontrollers with **Arduino & Embedded C++**.
- 🐧 **Linux & Desktop Environments:** Crafting **[Atom-Shell](https://github.com/aToom13/Atom-Shell)**, a portable and reliable desktop shell for Hyprland.
- 🎯 **Goals & Collaboration:** Eager to collaborate on open-source tools, autonomous mobile robots, and exploring internship / engineering opportunities.
